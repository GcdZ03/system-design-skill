# Draft 2, 2026-10-24: fresh scenarios

Two new scenarios that didn't shape the skill. Each was run once without the skill (baseline) and once with draft 2, which ran over two turns: the request, then the reply "defaults".

The raw outputs are in [`runs/2026-10-24-draft2/`](runs/2026-10-24-draft2/).

Setup: fresh Claude Code subagents (Opus 5.5), n = 1 each, scored by the session that wrote the skill.

## Scenarios

4. **Theater ticketing:** "I volunteer for a community theater with a 300-seat venue. We want our own online ticketing instead of paying a third-party site: people pick specific seats on a seating map, pay by card, and get a ticket with a QR code that's scanned at the door. Popular shows sell out within an hour of going on sale. Can you design the system?"
5. **Greenhouse sensors:** "My family runs a few commercial greenhouses. We're adding about 400 sensors (temperature, humidity, soil moisture) that report readings every 10 seconds over Wi-Fi. We want a dashboard with live and historical charts, and a text alert if a greenhouse gets too hot or too cold, since that can kill a crop overnight. I'm the only developer. Design the system."

## Draft 2 changes

| Change | Result |
|---|---|
| At most 10 questions, rest under Assumptions | ✅ Both asked exactly 10, down from 18–19 in draft 1. Questions were system-specific (seat hold length, offline door scanning, "a dead sensor looks like a quiet night") |
| Ranked hard-to-reverse flags, config never counts | ✅ Hold length and the median rule were marked "No (config)". Both named the top 5 with a change cost, then listed the rest |
| Summary first | ✅ Both opened with a summary of 4–5 lines |
| Size sections to the system | ❌ **Didn't work.** With-skill designs ran ~2,900–3,000 words, against 1,900–2,450 for the baselines |

## Takeaways

- The scores carry over to scenarios the skill wasn't shaped on, so this isn't overfitting to the first three.
- The greenhouse baseline was already strong on failure modes (dead-man's switch, power), but skipped questions, RPO, triggers and reversibility. That's the same pattern as draft 1's baselines.
- The length goal isn't binding. The soft wording ("keep each section short") lost against the required sections. Possible fixes: a concrete word budget, or accept the length because the 5-line summary gives the quick read.
