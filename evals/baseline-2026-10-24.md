## What the baseline already does well (so the skill doesn't need to teach it)

- Right-sizing: a monolith plus Postgres, explicit "not building" lists
- UNIQUE constraints for idempotency (check-ins, reminder sends, deliveries)
- Background workers for slow work, Postgres as the queue
- At-least-once delivery with dedupe IDs (webhooks)
- Domain-specific risks: time zones, EXIF GPS stripping, SSRF

## Takeaway

The gaps are **process discipline**, not knowledge. The skill focuses on asking first, showing the arithmetic, setting RPO per data type, numeric triggers, and failure walks.

The full rule set lives in `references/checklist.md`.
