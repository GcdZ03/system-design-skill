Before designing, I need a few details that affect cost and whether seats can be sold or scanned twice. The [designing-new-systems skill](.agents/skills/designing-new-systems/SKILL.md) requires “Ask before you design”; proposed defaults below make this one round.

1. **Demand:** How many performances per year, and how many people might arrive online when sales open? **[60 performances/year; 1,000 visitors in the opening minute, including 300 actively choosing seats.]**
2. **Budget and maintenance:** What monthly budget is workable, and who can maintain it? **[US$100/month for hosting and services, excluding card fees; one technical volunteer, with no round-the-clock support.]**
3. **Payments:** Which country/currency, and is a card processor acceptable? **[Australia/AUD; use a processor’s hosted checkout. Processing fees still apply.]**
4. **Ticket rules:** Do you need box-office sales, concessions, accessible-seat restrictions, season passes, refunds, or exchanges? **[Adult/concession prices, staff-managed accessible seats and complimentary tickets, box-office sales sharing online inventory, full-order refunds; no exchanges or passes initially.]**
5. **Seat holds:** How long should customers have to complete checkout? **[10 minutes; up to 8 seats per order; no customer account required.]**
6. **Door scanning:** How many scanning devices, and how reliable is venue internet? **[3 volunteer phones; internet with a mobile hotspot backup; a printable door list for a complete outage.]**
7. **Failure tolerance:** Is temporarily stopping sales preferable to risking lost bookings or double sales? **[Yes; preserve confirmed bookings, target recovery within 1 hour, and keep door admission possible during an outage.]**

**Assumptions:** One venue with a fixed 300-seat map; each performance has separate inventory; tickets arrive by email with one QR code per seat; volunteers need a password-protected administration area.
