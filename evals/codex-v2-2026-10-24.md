## Notes

- **The new model went much deeper.** It added separate card authorization and capture, a provisional offline admission lane, and a cross-region disaster recovery trigger. All of it is reasonable, but it's dense and jargon-heavy for a volunteer theater with one developer. `DESIGN.md` doubled in length while the chat hand-off stayed short, so the file/chat split held up.
- **Currency was consistent this time.** It used Australian dollars throughout, where v0.155.1 asked in AUD and priced in USD. No `AGENTS.md` or other instructions were found outside the repo, so the AUD default is the model's own choice.
- **Possible gap in the skill: no depth limit on `DESIGN.md`.** The skill sizes the *architecture* to the load, but not how much *detail* goes in the doc. Worth watching. It hasn't hurt any score.
